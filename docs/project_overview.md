# Project overview

## Mission

Help the client reduce **out-of-stocks (OOS)** in its distribution network and understand where
inventory management can improve, in a **demand-driven (DDMRP)** planning environment.

## Objectives

- Build a reliable, documented view of the data shared by the client and its distributors.
- Identify the drivers of OOS: demand variability, replenishment parameters, lead times,
  order fulfillment, distributor practices, product characteristics, data quality.
- Assess how DDMRP parameters (buffers, ADU, lead times, MOQ) relate to OOS performance.
- Recommend practical operational improvements.

## Stakeholders

| Group | Role |
|---|---|
| The client's planning / S&OP team | Project sponsor, data provider, final audience |
| Distributors A–D | Sources of inventory, order and shipment data |
| 116th Consulting (Columbia) | Analysis and recommendations |

Client contact names are deliberately not stored in this repository.

## Current scope

- **In scope now:** data understanding, exploration, quality assessment, cleaning, standardization.
- **Next:** OOS diagnosis, DDMRP / inventory analysis, recommendations.
- **To be confirmed with the client:** exact OOS definition, unit of analysis
  (e.g. SKU × market × distributor), time period, success criteria. See `open_questions.md`.

## Expected analytical outputs

- Data inventory and data dictionary per distributor
- Data quality assessment and documented cleaning rules
- Standardized cross-distributor dataset
- OOS diagnostic (where, when, which products, likely causes)
- Analysis of replenishment parameters vs OOS
- Prioritized recommendations

## Intended business impact

Fewer stockouts and better service to customers, without simply adding inventory: the goal is
to place the right inventory in the right place, with parameters that reflect real demand and lead times.
